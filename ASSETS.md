# Imagen del portal

`condor_app/views/static/images/andes-base.png` es una imagen original de un campus ficticio. Se generó con la herramienta integrada **imagegen**, sin fotografías de instalaciones militares reales ni emblemas oficiales.

Prompt final utilizado:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide website hero background for Cociber CTF, a fictional academic military training portal called Operación Cóndor set in Ecuador. Create a high-end cinematic but credible photograph-style panoramic landscape, 16:9, of an entirely imaginary modest training campus nestled in the Ecuadorian Andes. Snow-capped volcanic mountains, green highland valley, tasteful modern low-rise buildings, a courtyard and a small Ecuadorian tricolor flag. Late afternoon golden sunlight, natural textures, professional institutional photography, muted olive and slate colors. Composition: mountains across the upper background, campus in right lower half, darker uncluttered vegetation and shadowed hillside on the left so white website text can be overlaid later by HTML. Calm educational setting, no combat, no weapons, no real military unit insignia, no official government seal, no identifiable real base, no people close up. No written text, no embedded interface, no logo, no watermark. This is an original fictional website illustration.
```

El escudo del cóndor es un SVG creado para este ejercicio. Ninguno de los dos recursos representa una unidad oficial.
